About Trill

The Michelin Guide
for crypto KOLs.

A curated directory of credible voices in crypto, evaluated by an anonymous inspectorate, re-rated quarterly, and accountable to the institution behind it.

Why we exist

Crypto Twitter is a firehose. Bots, paid promotion, and self-declared experts make the signal-to-noise ratio close to zero. Existing solutions — mindshare-based leaderboards, popularity scores — reward the wrong thing: volume, not substance. The result is a market where loud voices crowd out careful ones.

Trill takes a different approach: institutional trust instead of personal ranking. Like the Michelin Guide, what readers trust is not any single critic but the institution itself — its standards, its inspectors, its accountability over time.

Three principles that shape everything

① Designation, not score

No numbers. Only Stars and Distinctions.

We never show 0.65 or 75% or "Trust Score 87". Numeric scores create false precision and trap users in meaningless "I'm 3 points higher than him" comparisons. We publish 1-5 Stars + Distinguished badges. Same as Michelin.

② Guide, not Leaderboard

We curate. We don't rank.

There is no "#1 Trill creator" — just as there is no "#1 Michelin restaurant in Paris". Mindshare data exists in our backend, but it never gets published. Public visibility is reserved for what's been evaluated and earned.

③ Institution as trust unit

Trust the Guide, not the individual.

A creator's rating moves over time. A bad call doesn't break trust in the Guide — the institution's standards, inspectors, and quarterly re-rating cycle are what's trusted. No single voice is a permanent authority.
