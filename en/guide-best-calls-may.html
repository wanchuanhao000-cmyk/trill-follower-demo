Best Calls of May 2026 — Receipted, Ranked, Resolved

Every call was made on record, against a counter-take, and has now resolved. Here are the five most distinguished of the month.

By The Trill Guide Editorial · 12 min read · Published May 26, 2026

A "best call" on the Guide isn't a screenshot. It's a public, dated thesis posted before consensus formed, defended against a visible counter-take, and resolved by an outcome the market itself rendered. Five of those happened in May. Here they are, in the order our Receipts ledger ranks them.

Methodology refresher: every entry below cites the call timestamp, the counter-take it stood against, the resolution event, and the dimension it strengthened on the creator's record. Anything we can't verify, we don't publish.

① Hsaka — Restaking risk concentration, the second leg

H

Hsaka ★★★

@hsakatrades · Strengthened: Analytical Depth, Accuracy Discipline

Called: May 4, 2026 — "If a single LST captures >40% of restaking flow, slashing correlation is the second-order risk, not yield compression."
Counter-take: Multiple distinguished researchers framed it as a yield-compression story.
Resolved: May 22 governance vote on caps. Hsaka's framing was adopted verbatim in two proposals.

This wasn't a price call. It was a framing call — and framings, when they win, change what every other voice in the conversation has to address. The Guide tracks these because they are the highest-leverage form of intellectual capital a creator can deploy.

② Tetranode — The $SOL January retrace

T

Tetranode ★★★

@tetranode · Strengthened: Track Record, Intellectual Honesty

Called: January 9, 2026 — "$SOL to $200 before $300. The structural liquidity isn't there yet."
Counter-take: Loudly dunked on by mid-tier CT for four months.
Resolved: May 11, $SOL printed $197 intraday. Tetranode posted a single-line retrospective and did not victory-lap.

The Guide weights "did not victory-lap" the same way it weights "called early." Intellectual Honesty isn't only about admitting being wrong — it's about not extracting social rent when right.

③ Vance — Restaking primer accuracy clause

V

Vance ★★★

@vance_research · Strengthened: Educational Value, Accuracy Discipline

Counter-take: The spec was treated as final by most secondary coverage.
Resolved: May 19 protocol announcement revised accrual exactly along Vance's flagged axis.

④ Cobie — The macro pause

C

"One distinguished call doesn't make a star. Twenty distinguished calls, defended against counter-takes, resolved on the public record — that does."

What didn't make the list

A reminder of what the Guide does not count as a "best call":

① Anything posted after the fact. If the trade is up and the post is fresh, it's not a call.
② Predictions without a counter-take. "BTC up" is not a thesis if nobody disagrees.
③ Anything we cannot resolve. Open positions stay in Receipts but don't enter Best Calls until rendered.
④ Calls promoted by a paid distribution stack. Distribution doesn't strengthen a record. The call has to stand on its own.
