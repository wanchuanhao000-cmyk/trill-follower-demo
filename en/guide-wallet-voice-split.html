Why this matters

The Trill Guide doesn't moralise about disclosure timing. Distinguished creators have earned the right to a longer reasoning cycle in public than the average reply-guy timeline. But for a follower trying to read the room, knowing that four of your most trusted voices have opened positions while the conversation stays cool is information you cannot get from a feed alone.

"Public conviction lags private conviction by exactly the time it takes to feel sure. The Guide just shows you both clocks."

How to read the Wallet/Voice Split

Three reading patterns we see repeatedly:

Pattern A — Quiet accumulation. The creator is bullish but waiting to be sure before posting. Often the highest signal — distinguished voices don't accumulate carelessly.

Pattern B — Disagreement with own thesis. The creator's public stance is cautious; the position contradicts it. Usually means the thesis is updating in real time.

Pattern C — Hedge or counter-trade. The creator is publicly long the narrative but their wallet is the opposite. Rare among Three-Star voices; common in lower tiers.

This week's four mismatches all read as A or B. None look like C.
