Methodology

How Trill evaluates

The rigor behind every Star, every Distinction, and every Re-rating.

The 90 / 10 composition

Every Trill rating is composed of two layers:

90%
Algorithm

Quantitative measurement of every public call: accuracy on resolved positions, depth of reasoning, intellectual honesty in self-correction, and 4 other dimensions — all measured from on-record behavior over time.

10%
Sentinel review

Human judgment from anonymous, staked, vetted inspectors. They review edge cases, catch what the algorithm misses, and contribute the "Community Confidence" dimension.

The 7 dimensions

Every evaluated creator is rated across seven distinct dimensions. We never publish numeric scores — only qualitative tiers (Distinguished / Strong / Standard / Developing).

①
Analytical Depth

Depth and substance of reasoning. Multi-framework analysis, evidence-based, not surface-level takes.

②
Intellectual Honesty

Willingness to self-correct, acknowledge mistakes, update views with new evidence. The opposite of double-down behavior.

③
Accuracy Discipline

Calibration of calls over time. Measured on resolved positions across 3 sub-factors: resolved-call accuracy, resolved volume, cross-cycle consistency.

④
Educational Value

How well the creator helps others learn. Explainers, primers, pedagogical depth.

⑤
Community Confidence

Qualitative read from the Sentinel inspectorate. The 10% human-judgment layer.

⑥
Entertainment

Engagement quality. Holds attention without resorting to noise. Distinguishes substance from spectacle.

⑦
Community Care

How responsibly the creator treats their audience. No shilling, no exit-pumping, no exploitative behavior.

Star tier thresholds

The 7 dimensions compose into a single overall designation — 1 to 5 Stars. Distribution is engineered to mirror Michelin's scarcity: most evaluated creators land at 3★, with the upper tiers reserved for those who excel across the board.

★★★★★ Composite ≥ 0.75 · Exceptional across multiple dimensions
★★★★★ 0.60 – 0.75 · Consistently strong
★★★★★ 0.45 – 0.60 · Reliable signal in their domain
★★★★★ 0.30 – 0.45 · Listed, but with notable gaps
★★★★★ < 0.30 · Below standard for the Guide

Underlying composite scores are never shown to readers. Stars only.

Distinctions

A creator can be Distinguished in a specific dimension regardless of their overall Star tier — analogous to Michelin's Special Awards. A 3★ creator can be Distinguished in Educational Value even if they're Standard elsewhere.

This matters because it lets readers find the right voice for what they need. Looking for honest self-correction in DeFi? Filter by "Distinguished in Intellectual Honesty" — Star tier becomes secondary.

Sentinels — the inspectorate

Sentinels are Trill's anonymous, vetted, staked human reviewers — analogous to Michelin inspectors. They join through a double-gate process:

Gate 1 · TrillClaw self-assessment

30-question quiz identifying archetype + fit Tier. Open to anyone, viral entry point.

Gate 2 · Engagement signal

Real behavior on Trill — watchlist longevity, profile reads, call tracking, audit refresh. Must cross 80% threshold.

Activation · Stake $TRILL

Tier-discounted stake locks in the inspector role. Reward multipliers + access to evaluation tooling.

Re-rating cadence

Every creator in the Guide is re-rated quarterly. Star movements (up or down) are shown publicly on each profile — "Re-rated 2026 Q1 ↑ from 3★". This matters: a Trill rating is never frozen, and the public record of movements is the institution's accountability.

Major movements trigger Watchlist alerts to followers. Removal from the Guide is announced with reason.

What we won't do

❌ Publish numeric scores

Numbers create false precision and "I'm 0.03 higher than him" comparisons. Designations only.

❌ Rank by mindshare / mention volume

Mindshare rewards loudness. We curate by Sentinel evaluation, not by who shouts most.

❌ Show "#1 / #2 / Top 10"

The Guide is a curated list, not a leaderboard. There's no #1 Michelin restaurant either.
