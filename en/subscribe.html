Quick FAQ

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — month-to-month plans cancel immediately; annual plans run until the end of the billing period, then auto-stop.

What's the difference between Pro Lite and Sentinel?

Pro Lite is a paid subscription for data depth. Sentinel is a role for evaluating creators — you stake $TRILL and contribute to The Guide. Most users want Pro Lite, not Sentinel.

Do I get DD Reports included with Pro?

Pro includes 1 DD Report per month. Extra reports are $49-199 depending on creator + depth.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime in Account → Subscription. Prorated automatically.
