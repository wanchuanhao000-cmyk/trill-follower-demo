一个维度是如何评估的

6 个维度,1 个综合,前台不显示单一数字。

Each creator is independently evaluated on all 6 个维度 by the Sentinel network. The composite produces a star tier (★ to ★★★); the dimensions themselves are what you browse by here. A ★★★ in Analytical Depth is a different thing from a ★★★ in 社区 Care — and the Guide is built so you can tell them apart.

No precise numerical scores are published on the public surface. The reason is structural: scores invite manipulation; designations invite trust. The Michelin Guide does not publish "the Beef Bourguignon scored 87.3." It publishes "two stars."
